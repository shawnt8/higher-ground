# Higher Ground

A plain-language flood decision explorer for a grandmother in Greenwell Springs, Louisiana, whose family reports six feet of water in her home in 2016. It compares staying, flood insurance, elevation, elevation with insurance, smaller improvements with insurance, and moving.

## Run locally

Serve `dist/` using any static web server, or open `dist/index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies are required. Google Fonts are optional; readable system fallbacks are included. All calculations run locally; no household inputs are sent to a server.

## Check the model

Run `node test-model.cjs`. The site includes linked sources, input ranges, assumptions and formulas. The model is an educational sensitivity comparison, not a parcel flood forecast. The family's reported flood depth is not independently verified. Property address and resident name are not displayed.

## Part 1 paragraph

Our decision-maker is a grandmother in Greenwell Springs, Louisiana, whose family reports that her house took on six feet of water during the 2016 flood. She must decide whether to stay without new protection, purchase flood insurance, elevate her home, combine elevation and insurance, make smaller protective improvements, or move to a lower-risk home. Our website lets the family explore how annual flood probability, changing risk, the planning horizon and financial assumptions affect cumulative flood exposure and household costs. It illustrates return periods, cumulative probability, hazard, exposure, vulnerability and climate adaptation while recognizing that safety, accessibility and community ties cannot be reduced to a dollar total.
